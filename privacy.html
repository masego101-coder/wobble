Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Welcome to WOBBLE Arcade! Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website, how it may be used, and what choices are available to you.

1. About WOBBLE Arcade

WOBBLE Arcade is an online gaming website offering free casual arcade games and brain challenges.

The website does not currently require visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to play its games.

For privacy-related questions, WOBBLE Arcade can be contacted using the email address provided in the Contact section below.

2. Information That May Be Processed

We do not currently ask visitors to submit personal information to access or play our games.

However, technical information may be processed when someone visits the website. Depending on the services involved, this may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages or resources requested.
  • Technical access logs and diagnostic information.

This information may be handled by website hosting infrastructure and other services involved in delivering the website. WOBBLE does not independently control all information processed by those providers.

3. Game Progress and Browser Storage

Some WOBBLE games use browser local storage to save information directly on your device.

This may include:

  • Game progress and unlocked levels.
  • Scores and collected coins.
  • Game preferences and settings.

This information is used to support game functionality and is generally stored in your browser rather than submitted to a WOBBLE user account.

Clearing your browser data or local storage may remove saved game progress. You can manage or clear this information through your browser settings.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

WOBBLE does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tracking scripts.

Browser local storage used to remember game progress is different from traditional cookies.

Third-party services involved in hosting or operating the website may use their own technologies and have separate data practices.

5. Website Hosting and Third-Party Services

WOBBLE Arcade is currently hosted using GitHub Pages, a website hosting service provided by GitHub.

When you visit the website, your connection and technical information may be processed by GitHub and its supporting infrastructure. The handling of this information is also subject to the relevant provider's privacy practices.

You can read GitHub's Privacy Statement .

6. Advertising

WOBBLE Arcade does not currently display advertisements or use Google AdSense advertising on the website.

Advertising services may be introduced in the future. If advertising is added, third-party advertising providers, including Google where applicable, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, measure performance and help prevent repeated advertisements.

Depending on the advertising settings and applicable requirements, advertisements may be personalised based on a visitor's previous visits to this or other websites.

Visitors can manage Google's personalised advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings .

Visitors can also learn more about certain online advertising choices at YourAdChoices .

If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant advertising services and explain their information practices and available privacy choices.

7. Children's Privacy

WOBBLE Arcade is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the games.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected in circumstances where it should not have been, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

8. Information Security and Retention

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information handled directly by WOBBLE against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Technical information held by third-party service providers may be retained according to their own security, operational and retention practices.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Where applicable under South African data protection law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), individuals may have rights relating to their personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of information where applicable.
  • Object to certain processing of personal information.
  • Raise a privacy concern or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To make a privacy-related request to WOBBLE, contact us using the email address below. We may need enough information to understand and respond to your request.

You can find information about the South African Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za .

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

WOBBLE Arcade may update this Privacy Policy as the website develops or introduces new features and services.

Changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

For questions, privacy requests or concerns about this policy or the handling of information, please contact WOBBLE Arcade:

wobblearcade@outlook.com

Information provided when contacting us will be used to respond to your enquiry and handle your request.
