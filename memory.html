How to Play Memory Blitz

Ready to put your memory to the test? Memory Blitz is a fun matching game where you flip cards, remember their positions and find all the matching pairs. The challenge is simple: remember what you've seen and match all eight pairs!

🎮 How to Play

  1. Start with a board of 16 face-down cards.
  2. Tap any card to reveal the emoji underneath.
  3. Tap a second card to try to find its matching pair.
  4. If the two cards match, they stay face-up.
  5. If they don't match, both cards turn face-down again after a short delay.
  6. Remember the positions of the revealed cards and use that information to find future matches.
  7. Continue until all eight matching pairs have been found.
💡 Remember: You can only reveal two unmatched cards at a time. Take your time and pay attention to what appears on the board.

📊 Understanding the Game Stats

Moves: Counts each attempt after you reveal your second card. One attempt consists of two card flips.

Time: Tracks how long you've been playing. The timer starts when you reveal your first card and stops when you find all eight pairs.

Pairs: Shows how many matching pairs you have successfully found out of eight.

🏆 Your challenge is to find all eight pairs using as few moves and as little time as possible.

🧠 Tips and Strategies

  • Pay attention: Remember the emoji and position of every card you reveal.
  • Build your memory: When two cards don't match, remember both positions for later.
  • Look for familiar cards: If you recognise an emoji from an earlier turn, use that information to find its partner.
  • Stay focused: Avoid rushing through your turns. A little concentration can save several moves.
  • Practice regularly: The more you play, the better you can become at remembering card positions.
  • Challenge yourself: Try completing the board in fewer moves or beating your previous time.

📋 Game Rules

  • The game contains 16 cards and eight matching pairs.
  • Only two unmatched cards can be revealed at a time.
  • Matching pairs remain face-up.
  • Incorrect pairs are hidden again after approximately 850 milliseconds.
  • You cannot select another card while an incorrect pair is being turned back over.
  • The game ends when all eight pairs have been matched.
  • You can restart the game at any time.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many cards are in Memory Blitz?

There are 16 cards arranged in four columns and four rows, containing eight matching pairs.

What happens when I find a matching pair?

Both cards remain face-up, and your pair count increases by one.

What happens when I choose two different cards?

Both cards are briefly displayed before turning face-down again. Try to remember their positions for your next turns.

When does the timer start?

The timer starts automatically when you flip your first card. It stops when you find all eight matching pairs.

Can I restart the game?

Yes! Select the Restart Game button above the board. The cards will be shuffled again, and your moves, timer and pair count will reset.

Does Memory Blitz save my progress?

No. The current version does not save individual game progress or scores. Each restart begins a fresh game.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes! Memory Blitz is designed to work on mobile phones as well as larger screens. Simply tap the cards to play.
