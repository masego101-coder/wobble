Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Welcome to WOBBLE Arcade! Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit and use our website, how it is used and what choices you have.

1. Introduction

WOBBLE Arcade is an online gaming website offering free casual arcade games and brain challenges. By accessing our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

WOBBLE does not currently require users to register for accounts or provide personal information to play its games.

However, certain technical information may be processed by services involved in operating the website, such as hosting providers. This may include IP addresses, browser information and technical access logs, depending on the provider's practices.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

Some WOBBLE games use browser local storage to save game progress, scores, coins and preferences directly on your device.

This information generally remains in your browser unless you clear your browser data or otherwise remove it. Clearing local storage may result in losing saved game progress.

4. Cookies and Tracking

WOBBLE does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tracking scripts.

Browser local storage used for game functionality is different from traditional cookies. Third-party services involved in hosting or operating the website may have their own technologies and data practices.

5. Third-Party Services

WOBBLE is currently hosted using GitHub Pages, a hosting service provided by GitHub. When you visit our website, your connection may be processed by GitHub and its infrastructure.

GitHub has its own privacy practices. You can read its Privacy Statement .

6. Advertising and In-Game Purchases

WOBBLE does not currently display advertisements or offer in-game purchases through an integrated payment system.

In the future, we may introduce advertising services, optional rewarded advertisements and optional purchases such as cosmetic upgrades, themes or additional game features.

If these services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant third-party providers, information processing and available choices.

7. Children's Privacy

WOBBLE is intended as a casual gaming website for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as WOBBLE develops or introduces new features and services.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions, requests or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, you can contact WOBBLE using the email address below.

Email: wobblearcade@outlook.com

We will use the information you provide when contacting us to respond to your enquiry.
