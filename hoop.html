🍩 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Tap a top hoop to select it, then tap a destination stick.
  2. You can also drag a top hoop between sticks.
  3. Move onto an empty stick or a stick with a matching top colour.
  4. Each stick has a maximum capacity. This grows as you progress.
  5. Sort every colour into a complete matching stack to win!

🪙 Complete levels to earn coins. Use coins for extra Undo, Switch Hoops and an extra empty stick.
