HOW TO PLAY

  1. Tap a stick to pick up its top hoop, then tap another stick to move it.
  2. Move hoops onto an empty stick or onto a hoop of the same colour.
  3. Sort each colour into its own stack to complete the level.
  4. You get 3 free Undo uses per level. Extra Undo costs 75 coins.
  5. Switch Hoops swaps two exposed top hoops. Add Stick gives you a temporary empty stick. Each costs 150 coins.
