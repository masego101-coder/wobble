🍩 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Drag the top hoop from one stick to another.
  2. You can move a hoop onto an empty stick or a matching colour.
  3. Use Switch Hoops to swap two exposed top hoops, even different colours.
  4. Add Stick gives you one extra empty stick for the current level.
  5. You get 3 free Undo uses per level. Further Undo uses cost 75 coins.
  6. Stick capacity grows as you progress: 4, 5, 6, then up to 7 hoops.
  7. Sort every colour into its own full stick to win!

🪙 Earn coins by completing levels. Power-ups cost coins; there are no real-money purchases.
