🍩 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Touch and hold the top donut on a stick.
  2. Drag it to another stick and release your finger.
  3. Move a donut to an empty stick or onto the same colour.
  4. Stick capacity grows as you progress: 4, 5, 6, then up to 7 donuts.
  5. Sort every colour into its own stick to win!

🪙 Earn coins by completing levels. Save 100 coins to unlock a sixth stick!
