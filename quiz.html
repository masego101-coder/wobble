How to Play Quick Quiz

Think you know a little bit about everything? Quick Quiz is your chance to put your general knowledge to the test! Answer questions across different categories, earn points and challenge yourself to beat your personal best.

🎮 Choose Your Game Mode

Endless Mode ♾️

  • Answer questions one after another.
  • You have 15 seconds to answer each question.
  • One incorrect answer or a time-out ends your run.
  • Keep going for as long as you can and build your score.

50-Question Challenge 🏆

  • Take on a full set of 50 questions.
  • Try to answer as many questions correctly as possible.
  • An incorrect answer does not end this mode.
  • Finish all 50 questions to complete the challenge.

Level Mode 🚀

  • Progress through 10 levels.
  • Each level contains five questions.
  • There are 50 questions in total.
  • Keep answering until you reach the final level.
💡 Choose the mode that suits your mood. Want a quick challenge? Try Endless Mode. Want to test your knowledge over a longer game? Try the 50-Question Challenge or Level Mode.

⏱️ How Questions Work

  1. Read the question displayed on the screen.
  2. Choose one of the four available answers.
  3. You have 15 seconds to make your choice.
  4. The correct answer will be highlighted after your selection.
  5. Read the feedback and select Next Question to continue.

If the timer reaches zero, the question is treated as an incorrect answer. In Endless Mode, this ends your run.

📊 Understanding Your Score

Points: Every correct answer earns 10 base points, plus the number of seconds remaining on the timer.

Correct: Shows how many questions you answered correctly.

Played: Shows how many questions you attempted during that game.

Accuracy: Displays the percentage of attempted questions you answered correctly.

Best Score: Your highest score for a particular game mode is saved in your browser.

🏆 Answer correctly and quickly to earn more points. Every second counts!

🧠 Tips and Strategies

  • Read carefully: Pay attention to the entire question before choosing an answer.
  • Watch the timer: You only have 15 seconds, so avoid spending too long on one question.
  • Use what you know: Think through the available options and eliminate answers that don't make sense.
  • Stay calm: Don't let the countdown make you panic.
  • Learn as you play: Take note of interesting questions and facts you discover.
  • Try different modes: Each mode gives you a different way to challenge yourself.
  • Beat your best: Return to your favourite mode and try to improve your previous score.

📋 Game Rules

  • Each question has four possible answers.
  • Only one answer is correct.
  • You have 15 seconds to answer each question.
  • Correct answers earn points based on your remaining time.
  • In Endless Mode, an incorrect answer or time-out ends the game.
  • The 50-Question Challenge and Level Mode continue after incorrect answers.
  • Level Mode has 10 levels with five questions each.
  • Your best score is tracked separately for each mode.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are available?

Quick Quiz has 50 questions covering different categories, including science, geography, mathematics, history, animals and general knowledge.

How much time do I get per question?

You have 15 seconds to select your answer. The countdown begins automatically when a question appears.

What happens if I get an answer wrong?

In Endless Mode, an incorrect answer ends your run. In the 50-Question Challenge and Level Mode, you can continue playing.

How are points calculated?

A correct answer earns 10 points plus the number of seconds remaining when you answer. Incorrect answers earn no points.

Does Quick Quiz save my best score?

Yes! Your best score for each game mode is saved in your browser using local storage. Clearing your browser data may remove these records.

Can I play again?

Absolutely! Select Play Again on the results screen or return to the mode selection screen to choose another mode.

Is Quick Quiz free to play?

Yes! Quick Quiz is free to play on WOBBLE ARCADE.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes! The game is designed to work on mobile phones and larger screens.
